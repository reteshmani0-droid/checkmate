# Credits

## Chess pieces

The twelve chess men in `src/piece-art.ts` are the **Cburnett** chess set, by Colin M.L.
Burnett, from Wikimedia Commons.

- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces>
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>

They are inlined as SVG so the board needs no font or image files. The drawings are
unmodified apart from how they are grouped in the source.

## Everything else

The interface, the board, the rules engine, the bot and the sounds were written for this
game. There are no third-party libraries.
